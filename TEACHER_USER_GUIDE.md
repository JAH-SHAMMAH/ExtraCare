# Teacher Quick Guide — Fairview School Portal

*Also in the portal: Teacher Guide in the menu.*

## 1. Start here
- Sign in with your school email and the password the office gave you. Change that password the first time you sign in (Profile or the prompt that appears).
- Turn on phone alerts: open Settings (the gear at the top) and switch on Phone Alerts, so you hear about notices even when the app is closed. On an iPhone, first add the portal to your Home Screen.
- Your menu shows only what your role allows. If a page below is missing from your menu, your role does not include it. Ask the office.

## 2. Every day: attendance
- Open School Attendance, then Mark Attendance. Pick your class and the date, then mark each pupil.
- Marking a pupil in or out sends their parents an alert straight away. Mark early so the arrival alert is on time.
- Subject lessons: View Subject Student Attendance under TimeTable shows who attended your subject.

## 3. Your classes and pupils
- My Classes lists the classes and subjects you teach, with today's timetable.
- Class List shows pupils in a class. You only see the classes you are assigned to.
- Behaviour Tracker and Manage Awardings let you record merits and incidents. Pastoral Remarks is for weekly remarks.

## 4. Lessons and classwork
- Lesson Planner: write your plan, save it as a draft, then submit it. Approve Lesson Plans is for the person who signs them off.
- Classwork (eClassroom) is where you give assignments and see what pupils hand in.
- CBT: build tests in Question Bank and Manage CBT, then read scores in Result Manager. Use Test Remark to mark written answers.

## 5. Marks and reports
- Enter scores in Gradebook or Exams & Results, or open Report Entry for the term's report grid.
- Add Teacher Comments and Skills & Behaviour for your class before the deadline.
- Class teachers press Submit on the Report Workflow page when the class is complete. After that the report is reviewed, approved and published by the office.
- When a class's reports are published, every parent gets an alert and can open the report card. Once published, scores are locked. Ask the office if a correction is needed.

## 6. Talking to families
- Communication Book (when the school has switched it on) lets you write a note to a pupil's family. They can read it, reply and tick that they have seen it.
- Messenger is for staff messages, and the News Feed is for notices to the school.
- Parents get phone alerts for arrivals, departures and published reports. They choose which alerts they want in their own Settings.

## 7. Good habits
- Mark attendance first thing, and enter marks as you go rather than at the deadline.
- Use the search box at the top to jump to a pupil or page quickly.
- Sign out on shared computers. Never share your password.
- Stuck? Tell the office what you were doing and what you saw on screen.
