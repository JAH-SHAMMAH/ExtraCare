# About This Guide

This is the administrator's manual for the **Fairview School Portal**, the school-management system built on ExtraCare. It is written for principals, administrators, and office staff — not for technical staff. There is no code or technical jargon here: just what each part of the system does, who is allowed to use it, and how to carry out the everyday tasks.

The guide describes the system **as it works today**. A small number of features are still being built. Wherever that is the case, the feature is clearly marked **"Not yet available"** so you always know what you can rely on.

**How to use this guide.** Every chapter follows the same pattern:

- **What it's for** — a plain description of the feature.
- **How to open it** — where to find it in the left-hand menu (the sidebar).
- **Who can use it** — which staff roles can see and use it.
- **Common tasks** — step-by-step instructions for the things you will do most often.

If you only read one other chapter, read **How Access & Permissions Work** next — it explains the roles referred to throughout, and it is the key to understanding why two members of staff may see different menus.

At the very end you will find a **Troubleshooting** chapter for common problems (such as an "access denied" message or a forgotten password) and an **Appendix** summarising what each role can do.


# How Access & Permissions Work

The portal shows each person only the parts of the system their **role** allows. This is why a teacher, an accountant, and a nurse all see different menus when they log in — each sees only what is relevant and permitted for their job. This keeps sensitive information (pay, medical records, exam answers) protected.

## The roles, in plain terms

Throughout this guide, "who can use it" is described using these everyday role names:

| Role | What they can generally do |
|---|---|
| **Super User** | Everything. The highest level of access in the school's portal. |
| **Administrator** | Principal, Head, and the main Admin account. Full access to school operations, fees, and user management. Does **not** automatically include the sensitive finance ledger and payroll unless they are also a Super User or Accountant. |
| **Senior Admin / Manager** | Vice Principal, Deputy Head, Administrative Coordinator, Head of Administration. Most administrative tools plus day-to-day fee handling. |
| **Teacher** | Teaching and academic staff. Lessons, classwork, marking, attendance, CBT tests, gradebook. No access to finance, HR administration, or system settings. |
| **Accountant** | The school's finance operator. Fees, the accounting ledger, payroll, and budgets. No access to academics. |
| **Nurse** | The confidential Medicals area only. |
| **Facilities** | The Facility Management area only. |
| **Cashier / Storekeeper / Front-Desk Officer** | Store and till sales, and viewing fees. |
| **Librarian** | The Library area only. |
| **HR Manager** | The HR area only. |
| **Student** | Their own timetable, library, classwork, CBT tests, and voting. |
| **Parent** | Their own children's attendance, report cards, fees, and feedback. |

## A few important points

- **A person can hold more than one role.** For example, a Principal may also act as the Accountant. Their menu combines everything their roles allow.
- **The most sensitive areas are separated on purpose.** Fee collection is one kind of access; the accounting ledger, payroll and budgets are a stricter kind, reserved for the **Super User, Accountant, and Head of Administration**. So a manager who can record a fee payment still cannot open payroll.
- **If a menu item is missing, it is not a fault** — it means the signed-in role does not have permission for it. See the Troubleshooting chapter.
- **Some staff can switch their "active role"** from the account menu (top-right) to temporarily work with a narrower set of permissions. This is separate from the "View as" preview that administrators can use to see the portal through another role's eyes.


# Getting Started

## Logging in

1. Open the school portal in a web browser using the school's web address.
2. Enter your email address and password, then sign in.
3. If you cannot sign in, contact your school administrator — they can reset your password (see Troubleshooting).

## Finding your way around

- **The sidebar** (left-hand menu) lists the areas you are allowed to use, grouped into sections. Click a section to expand it.
- **The dashboard** (the home screen) gives an at-a-glance summary for your role.
- **The top bar** holds search, notifications, the messenger, and your **account menu** (top-right), where you can view your profile, switch your active role if available, and log out.
- **Parents and students** see personal pages such as "My Children", "My Timetable" and "My Library" instead of the administrative menus.

## Everyday essentials

- **Messenger** and **News Feed** are available to everyone for school communication.
- **Notifications** (bell icon) alert you to things that need attention.
- Throughout the system, look for an **Add** or **New** button (usually top-right of a page) to create records, and the **pencil** (edit) and **bin** (delete) icons on each row.


# Admin Management

## What it's for
The administrator's control centre for looking after user accounts, passwords, biometric devices, and other school-wide housekeeping.

## How to open it
Sidebar: **Admin Management**. It contains: Manage Deactivated Users, Manage Week Entries, Result Publish Helper, Manage User Roles & Password, Manage Biometric, and Manage Custom Field.

## Who can use it
**Administrators only** (Super User, Principal, Head, and the main Admin account). Some items require the specific ability to manage users or roles.

## Common tasks

### Reset a user's password or change their roles
1. Open **Admin Management → Manage User Roles & Password** (this opens the Users screen).
2. Find the person in the list (use search).
3. Open their record, set a new password or adjust their assigned roles, and save. The person can sign in with the new password immediately.

### Re-activate a deactivated account
1. Open **Admin Management → Manage Deactivated Users**.
2. Locate the account and choose to reactivate it.

### Register or review a biometric (fingerprint) device
1. Open **Admin Management → Manage Biometric**.
2. Use the tabs to review devices, registered users (students and staff), attendance history, and device information. Add a device by entering its details in the Device Information area.

### Add a custom field
1. Open **Admin Management → Manage Custom Field**.
2. Create a new field (for example an extra piece of information you want to record about students), choosing its name and type.

> **Not yet available: Manage Week Entries.** This item appears in the menu but its underlying feature is still being built. It does not do anything yet.


# Students & Admissions

## What it's for
The complete pupil lifecycle: enquiries and admissions, the student roster, entrance exams, acceptance, and moving pupils between classes or out of the school.

## How to open it
Sidebar: **Students** (Students, Manage Withdrawal, Withdrawal List, Manage Inactive Students, Manage Students Pickup, Admissions & Enquiries, Enquiry Appointment, Entrance Exams, Post Entrance Form, Acceptance Form, Promotion Manager, Transfer Manager).

## Who can use it
**Admission Officers and administrators** for admissions and enquiries. Changing the roster (promotion, transfer, withdrawal) requires the ability to edit students, held by administrators and senior admin staff. Teaching staff can view the student list.

## Common tasks

### Add or find a student
1. Open **Students → Students**.
2. Use search to find a pupil, or use the **Add** button to create a new student record and complete their details.

### Record an admission enquiry and book an appointment
1. Open **Students → Admissions & Enquiries** and add the enquiry.
2. Open **Enquiry Appointment** to schedule a visit or interview.

### Run an entrance exam and process acceptance
1. Open **Students → Entrance Exams** to set up and record the entrance assessment.
2. Use **Post Entrance Form** and **Acceptance Form** to progress successful candidates into enrolment.

### Withdraw or reactivate a pupil
1. Open **Students → Manage Withdrawal**, select the pupil, and record the withdrawal with a reason.
2. Withdrawn pupils appear under **Withdrawal List**; inactive pupils under **Manage Inactive Students**.

### Promote a class or transfer pupils
1. Open **Promotion Manager** to move a year group up at the end of the session.
2. Open **Transfer Manager** to move individual pupils between classes.

### Manage end-of-day pickup
1. Open **Students → Manage Students Pickup** to record who is authorised to collect each pupil.


# Academics: CBT (Computer-Based Testing)

## What it's for
Creating and running online tests that mark themselves, with a question bank, live test sittings, results, and follow-up tools.

## How to open it
Sidebar: **CBT** (CBT Setup, Admin Question Bank, Admin Test Manager, Admin Result Manager, Admin Intervention, Admin Test Remark, Admin Test Export, Admin Cbt Import, Admin CBT Reset, Live Classes).

## Who can use it
**Teachers, Exam Officers, and administrators.** The Question Bank and Result Manager (which reveal correct answers and every pupil's scores) are restricted to staff. Students only ever see the tests they are asked to sit.

## Common tasks

### Build a question bank
1. Open **CBT → Admin Question Bank**.
2. Add questions (you can also bring questions in using **Admin Cbt Import**).

### Create and publish a test
1. Open **CBT → Admin Test Manager** and create a test, choosing its questions, timing, and the class sitting it.
2. Publish it so eligible students can take it. Once results are published they feed into the gradebook.

### Review and adjust results
1. Open **CBT → Admin Result Manager** to see scores.
2. Use **Admin Test Remark** to re-mark, **Admin Intervention** to flag pupils needing support, and **Admin Test Export** to download results.
3. **Admin CBT Reset** clears a pupil's attempt so they can re-sit, if needed.

# Academics: Secondary School Report

## What it's for
The reporting system for secondary pupils — the report-card configuration, report viewing, entering results, and the approve/publish workflow. The Nursery and Primary report sections work the same way and share these tools.

## How to open it
Sidebar: **Secondary School Report** (Report Setup, Reports View, Report Entry, Approve / Process Reports, Publish Report, Mark Books & Transcripts). There are matching **Nursery School Report** and **Primary School Report** sections.

## Who can use it
**Administrators** configure the report setup (this is a settings-level area). Report cards themselves are available to staff, and to parents for their own child.

## What works today

### Report Setup (partly available)
1. Open **Report Setup**. It is a tabbed configuration hub.
2. The following tabs are **live and usable now**:
   - **Terms & Sub-term** — define the school's terms (Autumn, Spring, Summer) and sub-terms (Half-Term, Full-Term), and mark which is currently active. Use **Seed** to create the standard set in one click, or **Add Term**.
   - **Comment** — define named comment slots for the report (for example a short "Classroom Behaviour" and a longer "Teacher's Comment").
   - **Result Default Comment** — pre-written comments that fill in automatically based on a pupil's score band and the teacher role.
   - **Term Begins/Ends Date** and **Attendance Setup** — for each term and sub-term, record the dates and the number of school days used to calculate attendance percentages.
   - **Deadline** — set the result-submission deadline for each term.

### Report cards
1. Open **Reports View** (or **Report Cards** under Academics) to view a pupil's report.
2. Report cards are generated automatically from the marks entered against pupils (see "How report cards are generated" in Troubleshooting).

### Approve and publish
1. **Report Entry** is where results are entered (this currently opens the **Exams** screen).
2. **Approve / Process Reports** moves a class's reports through the review stages.
3. **Publish Report** releases finished reports.

> **Not yet available (within Report Setup):** the remaining configuration tabs — **Grading System, Assessment Group, Assessment, Non Assessment Comment, School Motto/Seal & Sponsor, Result Type, Result Photo Setup, Subjects For Score Exclusion,** and **Result View** — are shown but are still being built. They display a "coming soon" style message.
>
> **Not yet available (Reports View charts):** the class **broadsheet** and the **Gender / Subject / Class performance charts** are planned but not built yet.
>
> In the meantime, grading and report formatting are managed under **School Setup → Report Config** (see the School Setup chapter).

# Finance

## What it's for
The school's money: fee collection, invoices, discounts, the school store, and the sensitive back-office accounting — chart of accounts, budgets, payroll, salary advances, petty cash, bank transactions, financial statements and reports.

## How to open it
Sidebar: **Finance** (Chart Of Accounts, Account Numbers, Accounts Setup, Manage Discounts, Invoice Center, Sales Monitor, Store Inventory, Warehouse, Store Pickup Unit, Store FrontDesk, Budget Mgt, Requisitions, Request Form, Direct Posts, Direct Transfer, Petty Cash, Salary Advance, Payroll, Bonus/Reduction Pack, Appointment Manager, Broad View, Fin. Statements, Cash Transactions, Reports, Fee Assignment, Fee Management, Payment Gateways, Billing & Subscription). Some fee tools also appear under **Operations**.

## Who can use it — two levels
Finance is deliberately split into two levels of access:

- **Fee collection and store sales** (Fee Management, Invoice Center, Discounts, Store, Petty Cash, Cash Transactions): **administrators, managers, and cashier/store roles**.
- **Sensitive accounting** (Chart of Accounts, Budgets, Payroll, Salary Advance, Direct Posts/Transfers, Financial Statements, Broad View, Reports): **Super User, Accountant, and Head of Administration only.** These are kept apart so that being able to take a fee payment does not give access to the ledger or payroll.

## Common tasks

### Assign and manage fees
1. Open **Finance → Fee Assignment** to set which fees apply to which classes.
2. Open **Fee Management** to review and manage the fees themselves.

### Record a fee payment and issue an invoice
1. Open **Invoice Center** to create and manage invoices.
2. Record payments against invoices; parents can also view and pay their child's fees from their own login.

### Apply a discount
1. Open **Manage Discounts** and record the discount for the pupil or fee (discount approval follows the school's process).

### Run payroll (sensitive)
1. Open **Finance → Payroll** (Accountant / Head of Administration / Super User only).
2. Prepare the payroll run. **A two-person rule applies: the person who approves a run cannot be the person who created it.**

### Review the school's finances (sensitive)
1. **Broad View** gives a wide financial overview.
2. **Fin. Statements** and **Reports** provide statements and detailed reports.

# Pastoral & Welfare

## What it's for
The pastoral, boarding, behaviour, and welfare centre — a pastoral dashboard, boarding/hostel life, merit points and awards, disciplinary cases, exeat (leave-of-absence) requests, mentor reports, and a pastoral report per pupil.

## How to open it
Sidebar: **Pastoral & Welfare** (Pastoral Dashboard, Pastoral Setup, Pastoral Students, Point Entry, Points Analysis, Pastoral Care, Photo Journals, Weekly Remarks, Merit & Awards, Hostel / Boarding, Hostel Students, Hostel Life, Hostel Reports, Pastoral Report, Exeat Requests, Mentor Reports, Behaviour & Sanction).

## Who can use it
**Pastoral and boarding staff, and administrators.** Boarding pages use a boarding permission; points, awards, and discipline use a behaviour permission.

## Common tasks

### Set up the pastoral module
1. Open **Pastoral Setup** — a tabbed hub covering hostel setup (managers, life grades, comment bank), houses, disciplinary setup (sanction groups, actions, committees), leadership roles, pastoral heads, exeat settings, roll call, point and award systems, and default settings.

### Award or deduct merit points
1. Open **Point Entry**, choose the pupil, the point type and term, enter the points (use a negative number for a demerit), and record it.
2. **Points Analysis** shows each pupil's points broken down by term.

### Manage boarding
1. **Hostel / Boarding** manages boarding houses and bed allocation.
2. **Hostel Students** lists boarders (with a bulk import). **Hostel Life** records per-boarder comments and grades. **Hostel Reports** holds daily and manager reports.
3. **Pastoral Roll Call** (in Pastoral Setup) marks boarders present/absent per session.

### Handle discipline and exeat
1. **Behaviour & Sanction** records disciplinary cases (offence, sanction, committee, status).
2. **Exeat Requests** manages a boarder's request to leave campus; approving an exeat is a safety-sensitive action reserved for administrators.

### Build a pastoral report
1. **Pastoral Report** brings together a pupil's points, hostel life, discipline, and remarks; you can add a termly remark using the remark bank.

# Troubleshooting

## "You do not have access" / "Required permission …"
This message means your signed-in role does not have permission for that page — it is not a fault.
- Check the account menu (top-right): are you signed in as the right person, and is your **active role** the one you need? If you switched to a narrower role, switch back.
- If you genuinely need access, ask a **Super User or administrator** to add the appropriate role to your account (Admin Management → Manage User Roles & Password).

## A menu item I expected is missing
The sidebar only shows what your role permits. If an item is missing, your role does not include it — ask an administrator to review your roles. (A very small number of items are still being built and are marked "not yet available" in this guide.)

## How report cards are generated
Report cards are built automatically from the information already in the system — you do not type the card by hand:
1. **Marks** are entered against pupils through **Exams & Results / Gradebook** and **CBT** (published results feed in automatically).
2. Each subject's score, grade, class position, and averages are calculated from those marks.
3. **Attendance** and the **comments** (class teacher / head teacher) come from the report details and the comment settings.
4. Staff then **Approve / Process** and **Publish** the reports; parents and pupils see the published card.
If a report looks empty, the usual cause is that grades have not been **published**, or marks have not been entered for that term.

## Resetting a password
1. An administrator opens **Admin Management → Manage User Roles & Password** (the Users screen).
2. They find the person, set a new password, and save. The user can sign in immediately with the new password.
(For security, the system does not email passwords; an administrator sets them directly.)

## A page says "not found" after a recent update
When the system has just been updated, both the main system and the screen you use are refreshed separately. If a brand-new page or menu item briefly shows "not found," it usually means the update is still being applied — wait a short while and try again, or ask your administrator to confirm the update has finished.

## Parents or students can't see something they should
Parents and students only see their **own** information (their children / their own timetable, fees, report card). If a parent cannot see a child, check that the parent is correctly linked to that pupil in the pupil's record.

## Sensitive finance areas are hidden
Payroll, budgets, the accounting ledger, statements and reports are restricted to **Super User, Accountant, and Head of Administration**. Holding fee-collection access is not enough — this separation is intentional.


# Appendix: Role Reference

This table summarises, in plain terms, what each role can typically reach. A person may hold more than one role, in which case their access combines.
